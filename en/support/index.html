01

For beta issues, start with TestFlight

Open TestFlight, select this app under its displayed name and use its built-in Send Beta Feedback option, or take a screenshot in the beta app and use the available beta-feedback option. Labels may vary by system and language. If built-in feedback is unavailable for your testing group, use the support email.

02

Help us reproduce the issue

Include the app version and build, device model, iOS / iPadOS version, Photos or Files source, photo or RAW type, steps, expected versus actual result, and whether it happens every time. Mark anything you do not know as “unsure.”

03

Screenshots and samples are always optional

A written description is enough to start. Feedback does not automatically read your photo library, and the app does not automatically upload photos, RAW files, projects or diagnostics to the publisher. Send a small number of screenshots or RAW samples only if you choose and have permission to share them. A complete library is not needed. Hide account, personal or location information and remember that originals may contain EXIF. We do not automatically collect or attach diagnostic files, and do not ask for passwords, verification codes or private keys.

04

How support material is handled

Support is handled manually by the publisher, for support, troubleshooting and app improvements. Ordinary records are kept up to 90 days after closure; open cases with no substantive correspondence for 180 days are ended and cleared. Original photos, RAW files, projects and detailed diagnostic copies are deleted within 30 days after analysis and no later than 90 days after receipt. Provider logs and backups follow their own rules.
